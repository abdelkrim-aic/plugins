## 1. Penalty clause — Strategic — source: “Penalty clause”

**Rule:** Delete any reference to a penalty clause. Look for automatic compensation, a fixed/lump sum or liquidated damages for a confidentiality breach. The source's unacceptable example requires EUR 50,000 for any breach while preserving claims for greater damages.

**Review application:** A smaller amount, mutual wording or a cap does not satisfy the deletion instruction. Distinguish fixed breach payments from ordinary proven-damage remedies. An injunction alone is not a penalty clause.

**Edit:** Delete the fixed-payment provision and dependent references. Preserve unrelated remedies unless they separately conflict with the worksheet. Do not invent a replacement payment amount.

## 2. Indirect damages — Strategic — source: “Indirect Damages”

**Rule:** Exclude liability for loss of profit and indirect or consequential loss, including special loss. Read references to indirect damages/loss, consequential damages/loss and special damages/loss carefully. The source rejects a clause making the parties liable for indirect damages and loss of profit.

**Source acceptable wording:**

> The Parties shall be entitled to equitable relief by way of injunction or other appropriate remedy to redress a breach or threatened breach hereof, without prejudice to any other rights or remedies otherwise available. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Parties shall under no circumstances whatsoever be liable for any loss of profit, or any indirect or consequential loss (including but not limited to special loss).

**Review application:** An effective exclusion passes; the mere presence of these words is not a breach. Inspect indemnities and exceptions that reinstate excluded liability. If the NDA is silent, flag the missing express protection and propose the source exclusion; do not assert that silence proves indirect liability under the governing law. The source's broad statement about French law is not a substitute for current legal verification.

## 3. Applicable law and arbitration — Legal — source: “Applicable Law and arbitration”

**Rule:** Identify both the governing law and the dispute forum. The explicit unacceptable combination is **law outside the EU + courts outside the EU**. The source values arbitration for confidentiality and avoidance of local-court concerns, especially with non-EU law, but supplies no acceptable model clause.

**Review application:** Preserve the conjunction: the worksheet does not prohibit every non-EU element independently. Do not treat EU law with non-EU courts, non-EU law with EU courts, or non-EU law with arbitration as automatically prohibited or automatically approved. Explain the combination and raise any unresolved legal risk. Missing or contradictory law/forum provisions need resolution. Verify current EU membership if classification is uncertain.

**Edit:** Propose selecting appropriate EU governing law and an agreed dispute forum, or an arbitration arrangement for legal review. Do not invent a mandatory French-law, Paris-court, or ICC preference. When details are undecided, show explicit bracketed choices for law, forum, and, if arbitration is selected, institution/rules, seat and language. Label such drafting as incomplete pending those choices, not signature-ready or workbook-approved.

## 4. Bilateral obligations — Commercial — source: “Bilateral”

**Rule:** Obligations should be bilateral when both parties disclose confidential information. A unilateral NDA is allowed when GeoPost either does not provide confidential information or does not receive it.

**Review application:** Determine actual information flows. If both disclose, check the operative obligations throughout the agreement, not just the title or opening paragraph. If only one discloses, ensure that party receives protection. If flows are unknown, ask rather than assume all unilateral NDAs fail.

**Source acceptable wording:**

> In connection with those discussions, it will be necessary for the Disclosing Party to make available to the Receiving Party information about the Disclosing Party, its affiliates and the Project. This Agreement describes the basis upon which each Party will hold and use that information.

**Edit:** Where mutual protection is needed, define each party as Disclosing Party or Receiving Party according to its role in each disclosure and align all confidentiality/use obligations. Changing the recital alone is insufficient.

## 5. Purpose — Commercial — source: “Purpose of the NDA”

**Rule:** The purpose limits information sharing to a specific topic and must reflect the reality of the upcoming discussions. The source's acceptable example describes evaluating a particular collaboration concerning D2C European Service Implementation, project documentation and service organization expertise; those project details are illustrative, not defaults.

**Review application:** Compare the stated purpose and permitted use to the actual tender, negotiation or project. Flag missing, mismatched or unnecessarily broad wording. If the actual purpose is unavailable, identify the missing business fact and do not declare alignment.

**Proposed drafting pattern (not a source quotation):**

> The Parties may exchange Confidential Information solely to evaluate and discuss [specific project or proposed commercial relationship] (the “Purpose”). Each Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

Replace brackets only with confirmed facts and adapt to the NDA's existing terms.

## 6. IP rights — Strategic — source: “IP rights”

**Rule:** At the NDA stage, GeoPost SA does not accept use of DPDgroup intellectual property, including trademarks, logos and publicity, without GeoPost SA's prior written agreement. Disclosure must not confer IP rights. The unacceptable example grants the receiving party intellectual property rights over shared confidential information.

**Source acceptable wording:**

> It is expressly understood and agreed by the Parties that the disclosure and provision of Confidential Information under this Agreement by either Party to the other shall not be construed as granting to the receiving Party any rights whether express or implied by licence or otherwise on the matters, inventions or discoveries to which such Confidential Information pertains or any copyright, trademark or trade secret rights.

**Review application:** Check grants, licences, ownership transfers and publicity/logo permissions. Distinguish permission to evaluate information for the agreed purpose from a grant of IP rights. Do not assume silence grants rights; flag missing protection and propose clarification. Do not treat an unproduced alleged consent as established.

**Edit:** Remove conflicting grants and add/adapt the source reservation. If needed, propose an express prior-written-consent requirement for names, marks, logos and publicity. Label that added sentence as proposed drafting implementing the policy, not a verbatim source clause.

## 7. Duration — Commercial — source: “Duration”

**Rule:** Favor **two years from signature**. The worksheet describes a usual range of two to five years, but expressly says **five years or more seems excessive** for the kind of information shared. The project manager may accept a shorter or longer period as a business decision; duration is not an automatic legal prohibition.

**Source acceptable wording:**

> This Agreement and its confidential obligations shall be effective as from the date of its signature and shall continue to be in force for a duration of two (2) years.

**Review application:** Assess agreement term and confidentiality survival separately, including time measured from each disclosure, termination, renewal and any indefinite survival. A two-year agreement with a further five-year confidentiality obligation is not equivalent to the preferred two years from signature. Flag indefinite or long-lived obligations for a business decision without asserting that they are unlawful. Do not automatically reject an expressly accepted commercial departure.

**Edit:** Propose aligning both the term and confidentiality obligations to two years from signature, adjusting inconsistent survival provisions. If the project owner accepts another duration, record the chosen period and rationale without claiming the worksheet requires it.
